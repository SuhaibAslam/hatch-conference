# Orient: Find the Judgment

Use this with the first two pages of the Personal Workbook when your team needs a concise reminder of the activity.

## Locate a real friction

Start with a situation from your own work, a colleague's work, or the experience of a user, customer or client. Name the task or decision and where AI support could make something important easier to miss.

## Inspect the response

Try the task with an AI tool or examine a plausible AI response. Capture what stands out:

- what the response handles well;
- what it assumes, flattens or leaves unclear;
- which decision could become worse if someone trusted it too quickly.

Mark the response when that is convenient. Notes in the workbook are enough when the response cannot be marked directly.

## Sharpen the judgment

State the consequence you see, then turn the critique into a rule a capable tool or teammate could follow. Keep the rule specific enough to guide another attempt.

Open [prompt--thinking-partner.md](prompt--thinking-partner.md) only when another perspective would help the team inspect the response or sharpen the rule.

## Leave Orient with

A recognisable situation, one consequence and one judgment rule.
