# Starting Challenge: Adaptive Onboarding

## Context

A product flow asks different questions based on previous answers. Completion has increased, while several people report that the product makes assumptions about them.

## Challenge

Design an adaptive flow that keeps its reasoning visible and gives people a practical route to correct it.

## Available material

- a current onboarding flow;
- prior answers and behavioural signals;
- completion and correction data;
- product constraints.

## Conditions to design

- People can see why a question or recommendation appears.
- People can edit or redirect an inferred answer.
- The team can detect repeated corrections and review the underlying assumption.

## Useful skill starting points

- [Mixed-initiative flow](../../../skills/mixed-initiative-flow/SKILL.md): decide what the product can infer, what it must show, and when people can correct or redirect it.
- [Constraint specification](../../../skills/constraint-specification/SKILL.md): make the permitted context, limits and desired output explicit.
