# Starting Challenge: Research Synthesis Assistant

## Context

A product team has 14 interview notes from customers, frontline staff and operations colleagues. It needs a one-page brief for a decision workshop tomorrow.

## Challenge

Prepare a synthesis that supports a decision without smoothing away disagreement or hiding missing evidence.

## Available material

- interview notes from customers, frontline staff and operations colleagues;
- a decision workshop agenda;
- a service-flow snapshot.

## Conditions to design

- The brief needs clear evidence and provenance.
- The team needs to see unresolved questions before it decides.
- A person remains accountable for prioritisation.

## Useful skill starting point

[Constraint specification](../../../skills/constraint-specification/SKILL.md): define source boundaries, traceability, the distinction between evidence and inference, and the decision the brief needs to support.
