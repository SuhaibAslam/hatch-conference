# Specify: Shape the Shared Work

Use the printed canvases as the team's working surface. This folder provides optional guidance, examples and a digital specification when they help.

## 1. Choose the challenge

Each person prepares one candidate challenge. Use earlier reflections as inspiration when useful, then choose a shared challenge with a clear person or team, situation, meaningful consequence and enough authorised material for a small trial.

Open [guidance--choose-a-challenge.md](guidance--choose-a-challenge.md) when the team needs help choosing. Use [examples--starting-challenges/](examples--starting-challenges/) only when no suitable real challenge is ready.

## 2. Look beneath the challenge

On Team Canvas 1, first note observations individually. Then bring them together and decide which needs, assumptions, risks, uncertainties and boundaries the later design must account for.

## 3. Shape the working relationship

Use Team Canvas 2 to describe how people and AI might address the challenge together. Decide the intended outcome, roles, scope, authority, visibility, control and recovery. Use [template--working-specification.md](template--working-specification.md) only when the team wants a digital copy.

## 4. Map and review the workflow

Use Team Canvas 3 to make the working specification visible as a flow. Then read Canvases 2 and 3 silently, add observations to Canvas 4 and agree what to keep or strengthen.

## Move on with

One selected challenge, a working specification, an inspectable flow and one recurring task, decision or judgment the team may carry into Encode.
