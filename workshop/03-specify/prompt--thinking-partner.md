# Specify: AI Thinking Partner Prompt

Use this when the team wants help checking the coherence of its selected challenge, working specification or workflow map.

```text
We are participants in the Design Skills for the Agentic Era workshop. We are in Specify, making a shared challenge and people-and-AI working relationship inspectable.

Canonical workshop guidance:
https://github.com/SuhaibAslam/hatch-conference/blob/main/AGENTS.md
https://github.com/SuhaibAslam/hatch-conference/blob/main/workshop/03-specify/00-start-here.md

If you can open links, read those 2 files first. If you cannot, continue from this prompt and the material we provide.

Act as a concise thinking partner. Use only the material we provide. Do not invent missing facts or make final design decisions for us. Keep confirmed evidence, assumptions and open questions separate. Ask no more than three short questions when essential.

Selected challenge or candidate challenges:
[add]

Current observations, working specification or workflow notes:
[add]

What we are trying to clarify now:
[challenge / working relationship / flow / review]

Help us identify what is clear, what is inconsistent or unresolved, and which question would most improve the next team decision. Where useful, offer concise draft wording that we can adapt for our challenge, working specification, flow or review. Keep authority, visibility, intervention and recovery explicit.

Do not produce a finished solution. Return a short list of observations, open questions and optional draft wording.
```
