# Specify: Choose a Challenge

Choose one shared challenge to carry through the rest of the workshop. It should have a person or team, a situation, a meaningful consequence, an open design question and enough authorised material for a small test.

## Prepare individually

Use Personal Workbook page 4 to prepare one candidate. Earlier reflections may inspire the choice, but the candidate can come from any suitable work you know.

## Decide as a team

Use Team Canvas 1 to choose one challenge. Prefer the option with the clearest consequence and enough material to test today.

## Use an example only when needed

Open [examples--starting-challenges/](examples--starting-challenges/) if no team member has a suitable real workflow. These are neutral practice material; replace them with real work whenever you can.

## Check before moving on

Can the team state the person or team, the situation, the meaningful consequence, the open challenge and the material it can use for a small test?
