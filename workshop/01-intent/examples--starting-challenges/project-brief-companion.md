# Starting Challenge: Project Brief Companion

## Context

A project team uses AI to turn early notes into a concise brief. The first drafts are fast, but they sometimes make untested assumptions sound settled or hide decisions that still need an owner.

## Challenge

Design AI support that helps a team prepare a clear project brief while keeping assumptions, open decisions and approval points visible.

## Available material

- meeting notes and research highlights;
- a previous project brief;
- project goals, constraints and delivery dates;
- named decision owners.

## Conditions to design

- The brief distinguishes confirmed decisions, assumptions and open questions.
- People can correct or reject an inferred commitment before it is shared.
- The team can see what information is missing and who needs to decide.

## Useful skills

- [Constraint specification](../../../skills/constraint-specification/SKILL.md): define the permitted context, required brief structure and limits on inference.
- [Consent and agency](../../../skills/consent-and-agency/SKILL.md): make approval and override practical before the brief becomes a commitment.
