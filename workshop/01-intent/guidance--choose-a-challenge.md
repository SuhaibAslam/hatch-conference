# Intent: Choose a Challenge

Choose one real workflow to carry through the workshop. It should have a person or team, a task or decision, a meaningful consequence, and enough authorised material for a small test.

## Start with the Personal Workbook

Individually, name one AI-related friction you recognise. Identify the distinction or human judgment that a smooth-looking AI output could miss.

## Decide as a team

Use Team Canvas 1 to choose one challenge. Prefer the option with the clearest consequence and enough material to test today.

## Use an example only when needed

Open [examples--starting-challenges/](examples--starting-challenges/) if no team member has a suitable real workflow. These are neutral practice material; replace them with real work whenever you can.

## Check before moving on

Can the team state the person affected, the task or decision, the consequence of a weak outcome, and the material it can use to test the work?
