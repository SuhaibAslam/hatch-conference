# Possibility: Agency and Control

Decide how people and AI should share the work before specifying the workflow in detail.

## Use the Personal Workbook

Map a meaningful workflow step. Decide whether the person leads, the AI leads, or they work together. Bring one disagreement about authority to your team.

## Decide together

Name:

- what the AI can prepare, recommend or do;
- what a person must decide or approve;
- what the person needs to know before acting;
- how the person can pause, edit, redirect or recover the work;
- what condition changes the level of AI initiative.

Use [mixed-initiative flow](../../skills/mixed-initiative-flow/SKILL.md) when control and handoff are the main question. Use [consent and agency](../../skills/consent-and-agency/SKILL.md) when permission, opt-out or override are central.

## Check before moving on

Can the team explain who leads each important step, what makes that appropriate, and how authority returns to a person?
