# Coordinate: Agency and Control

Decide how people and AI should share the work before specifying the workflow in detail.

## Use the Personal Workbook

Choose a broader workflow or task, then map three moments where who acts or decides matters. Select an agency mode for each moment and name what should shift that mode.

## Compare one choice with a partner

Each person shares one mapped moment. Compare:

- what the AI can prepare, recommend or do;
- what a person must decide or approve;
- what the person needs to know before acting;
- how the person can pause, edit, redirect or recover the work;
- what condition changes the level of AI initiative.

Use [mixed-initiative flow](../../skills/mixed-initiative-flow/SKILL.md) when control and handoff are the main question. Use [consent and agency](../../skills/consent-and-agency/SKILL.md) when permission, opt-out or override are central.

Open [prompt--thinking-partner.md](prompt--thinking-partner.md) when another perspective would help you test an authority choice or surface an assumption.

## Leave Coordinate with

Three mapped moments and one authority choice you see more clearly after the pair comparison. Keep the condition that would shift the mode visible in your Personal Workbook.
