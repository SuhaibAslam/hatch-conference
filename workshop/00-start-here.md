# Start Here

Use this folder in order. Its numbered folders follow the five states used throughout the workshop: Intent, Possibility, Definition, Action and Outcomes.

This repository is a support route, not another workbook to complete. The facilitator will flag the two core laptop-lab moments: **Action** for creating a skill and **Outcomes** for testing it. Use the earlier folders only when they help your team move forward.

The printed materials and these files work together, but they are not a one-to-one set:

- The **Personal Workbook** supports individual reflection, critique and agency choices.
- The **six Team Canvases** hold the team challenge, agreement, workflow map, peer review, guidance and comparison evidence.
- The **files in this folder** provide concise guidance, digital templates and copyable prompts where an AI tool is useful.

## How to read the files

- **Guidance** helps a team decide what to do next and points back to the printed work.
- **Template** gives a team a digital structure to complete or retain after the workshop.
- **Prompt** is optional copy-and-paste text for an AI tool; use it only after the relevant team decision is clear.
- **Skill** is a `SKILL.md` instruction an AI agent can load. If your tool does not load skills, paste the relevant skill with your task and template. [Tool setup](../tool-setup/) shows both routes.

## Where the repository fits

| Possibility | Personal Workbook and team discussion | Optional agency guidance when the team needs help deciding who leads. |
| Definition | Team Canvases 2 and 3 | Optional digital operating-agreement template for a team that wants to retain its decisions. |

## 01 Intent | Choose a challenge

Open [01-intent/guidance--choose-a-challenge.md](01-intent/guidance--choose-a-challenge.md) only when your team needs help choosing a challenge. Bring a real workflow if possible. Use [examples--starting-challenges/](01-intent/examples--starting-challenges/) only when your team needs practice material.

## 02 Possibility | Decide how agency works

Open [02-possibility/guidance--agency-and-control.md](02-possibility/guidance--agency-and-control.md) with the Personal Workbook and team discussion. Decide who leads, what a person needs to know, and when authority changes.

## 03 Definition | Make the agreement visible

Complete [03-definition/template--operating-agreement.md](03-definition/template--operating-agreement.md) alongside the Team Canvases. Then map the workflow from trigger to recovery.

## 04 Action | Write guidance

Complete [04-action/template--portable-skill.md](04-action/template--portable-skill.md) for your own challenge. The [skills/](../skills/) folder is an optional reference library: open a pattern only when it helps answer a specific design question, or keep it for later use. Use [prompt--draft-guidance.md](04-action/prompt--draft-guidance.md) only when you want AI help drafting the team's skill.

## 05 Outcomes | Test and improve

Open [05-outcomes/00-start-here.md](05-outcomes/00-start-here.md). It gives the default route in order: point the agent to [run-skill-trial](../skills/run-skill-trial/SKILL.md), review the named outputs in Canvas 6 or your shared workspace, then decide the revision with evidence. The agent can offer [comparison-desk.html](05-outcomes/comparison-desk.html), or create a standalone Desk, when the team wants an interactive review workspace. Use [template--controlled-comparison.md](05-outcomes/template--controlled-comparison.md) or [prompt--run-and-review.md](05-outcomes/prompt--run-and-review.md) only when the team needs a manual route. Use [template--skill-evaluation-plan.md](05-outcomes/template--skill-evaluation-plan.md) when you want to keep improving the guidance after the workshop.
