# Consent and Agency

## Use this when

The work depends on a person understanding, approving or being able to change what an AI system does with their information, choices or work.

## Make the agreement explicit

State:

- what information the system may use and why;
- what it may do, change, send or recommend;
- what needs permission or approval before it happens;
- what a person can edit, pause, redirect, undo or decline;
- when permission can be withdrawn and what happens next.

Ask for permission at the point it matters. A broad agreement at the beginning is not enough when the system later needs new information or wants to take a new kind of action.

## Keep authority with the person

Use clear, practical controls. People should be able to stop an action before it happens, correct an assumption, choose a different route or continue without AI support. Make consequential actions reversible where possible. When they are not reversible, make the approval point and consequence unmistakable.

## Check before you continue

Confirm that a person can answer:

- What is the system using?
- What will it do with it?
- What choice do I have now?
- How do I change or stop this?

## Workshop use

Use this with the working specification when deciding which choices stay with people, which boundaries the AI must not cross, and how people can question, correct or stop the work. It is especially useful for the adaptive-onboarding starting challenge.
