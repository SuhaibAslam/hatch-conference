Design Skills for the Agentic Era

Carry one real AI-supported workflow from a human judgment to guidance your team can test, compare and improve.

Begin with Intent

Working with an AI tool today?

Give it the workshop context once.

Start a new conversation, share only material your team is authorised to use, and tell the tool which workshop stage you are in.

01 Intent

What real work will we improve?

Choose one workflow where a weak AI-supported outcome would matter to a real person or team.

Choose together

  • PersonName who is affected by the work.
  • ConsequenceState what becomes harder, riskier or less trustworthy when the outcome is weak.
  • Test materialConfirm that you have enough authorised material for a small trial today.
Move on withOne challenge your team can state in a sentence and test with real or de-identified material.

02 Possibility

How should people and AI share the work?

Decide where the tool may take initiative and where a person must stay informed, intervene or decide.

Person leads

Use human judgment when consequences, context or accountability cannot be delegated.

AI leads

Let the tool prepare or act when the work is bounded, observable and easy to correct.

Work together

Make handoffs, checkpoints and changes in authority explicit.

Move on withA clear answer for who leads each important step, what changes that choice, and how authority returns to a person.

03 Definition

What agreement will govern the workflow?

Make the conditions visible before you ask an AI tool to perform the work.

PurposeWhat starts this, what good looks like, and who is accountable?
ContextWhat may the system use, and what must remain outside it?
ControlWhat may it do, what requires approval, and how can someone intervene?
RecoveryWhat happens when information is weak, the system is wrong, or the work must stop?
Move on withAn operating agreement the whole team can explain, challenge and use to map the workflow.

04 Action Core laptop lab

Turn one design judgment into a portable skill.

A portable skill is a set of reusable instructions that tells an AI tool how to apply one human design judgment consistently. Your team decides the judgment; the tool can help make the instructions clear.

01

Bring the team’s decision

Use Team Canvas 5 to name the workflow, the judgment worth protecting, allowed context, the human checkpoint and what a good output must demonstrate.

02

Structure the skill

Write when the guidance applies, what it needs, the procedure, constraints, checkpoint, output format, evaluation criterion and response to uncertainty.

03

Draft with your AI tool

After the team’s boundaries are clear, paste a structured request into your AI tool. Add your decisions where the prompt asks for them, then review every instruction together.

Leave Action withOne portable skill your team believes can improve the chosen task.Do not trust the draft yet. Outcomes is where you test whether it works.

05 Outcomes Core laptop lab

Does the skill improve the work?

Run the same task twice. The first output shows what the AI tool does without your skill. The second shows what changes when the skill is applied. Your team decides whether those differences matter.

Baseline outputThe task completed without your portable skill.

This is the starting point. Keep the task, input material and output format unchanged.

then
Guided outputThe same task completed with your portable skill.

Only the skill changes. That makes the comparison useful.

01

Prepare one fair task

Have your portable skill, task, authorised input material and required output format ready. Start a fresh AI conversation when possible.

02

Ask the AI tool to run both versions

The copied instructions run the baseline first, then apply your skill to the same task and return two clearly named outputs. They leave the scoring to your team.

03

Review evidence together

Keep both outputs visible. Cite the exact phrase, behaviour or omission behind each score, then record one improvement, one cost or regression, and one revision.

Optional review workspace

Use the Comparison Desk when side-by-side scoring will help.

It keeps both outputs, criteria, cited evidence and your revision decision together. You can complete the same review on Team Canvas 6 or in your shared workspace.

Open the Comparison Desk
Leave Outcomes withOne evidenced improvement, one cost or regression, and one next revision.

After the workshop

Keep testing the judgment and the skill across more cases.

Watch for regression and revise when the evidence changes.
